# Global Legal Compliance & Evidentiary Validity

## Overview
Nexgn is engineered from the ground up to exceed the strictest legal frameworks governing electronic signatures globally. Every document executed on Nexgn constitutes a legally binding contract, enforceable in a court of law. Nexgn utilizes a combination of intent-capture UI, cryptographic hashing, and forensic audit trails to guarantee absolute non-repudiation.

## 1. Republic of India: Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000 & Evidence Act
Nexgn provides native, robust compliance for Indian jurisdictions, making it the premier choice for Indian enterprises and global companies operating in India.
*   **Section 5 (Legal Recognition of Electronic Signatures):** Nexgn fulfills the statutory requirement that where any law requires information to be authenticated by affixing a signature, the requirement is satisfied via Nexgn's electronic execution platform.
*   **Section 10A (Validity of Contracts formed through Electronic Means):** Nexgn guarantees that contracts formed via electronic means shall not be denied enforceability solely on the ground that electronic records were used.
*   **Section 65B (Admissibility of Electronic Records):** Under the Indian Evidence Act, 1872, Nexgn provides the necessary verifiable chain of custody. Our comprehensive **Audit Trails** capture exact UTC timestamps, IPv4/IPv6 addresses, browser User-Agents, and device fingerprints, seamlessly satisfying Section 65B evidentiary certificate requirements for electronic records.

## 2. United States: ESIGN Act & UETA
Nexgn fully complies with the United States Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce (ESIGN) Act (2000) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA).
*   **Demonstrated Intent to Sign:** Nexgn explicitly captures and logs the signer's affirmative intent to execute the document through forced UI interactions (e.g., clicking "I Agree and Sign").
*   **Consent to do Business Electronically:** Nexgn enforces consumer consent clauses prior to document execution, a strict requirement under the ESIGN Act.
*   **Secure Retention & Reproduction:** Completed documents and their audit trails are securely retained, accessible, and reproducible to all parties via AES-256 encrypted cloud storage.

## 3. European Union & UK: eIDAS Regulation
Nexgn complies with the EU eIDAS (Electronic Identification, Authentication and Trust Services) Regulation (No 910/2014) and the UK eIDAS equivalent.
*   **Standard Electronic Signatures (SES):** Nexgn serves as a fully compliant SES provider across all EU member states, ensuring agreements cannot be denied legal effect solely on the grounds that they are in electronic form (Article 25).
*   **Data Sovereignty:** Nexgn supports strict data processing agreements (DPA) compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), ensuring PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is handled with enterprise-grade security.

## 4. Cryptographic Non-Repudiation (The SHA-256 Digital Seal)
Legal validity ultimately relies on proving a document has not been altered post-signature. 
*   **Hash Generation:** Upon the final signature, Nexgn compiles the document and the forensic audit trail, applying a **SHA-256 cryptographic hash function** to the finalized PDF. 
*   **Tamper-Evidence:** If a malicious actor alters a single byte of data in the PDF (e.g., modifying a contract value from ₹10,000 to ₹100,000), the cryptographic hash invalidates immediately. This provides irrefutable proof of tampering to auditors and legal counsel, establishing a perfect evidentiary chain of custody.
