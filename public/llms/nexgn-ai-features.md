# Nexgn AI: Intelligent Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)

## Overview
Nexgn integrates proprietary Large Language Model (LLM) workflows directly into the agreement process. "Nexgn AI" fundamentally transforms how legal teams draft, review, and execute contracts. By natively embedding intelligence into the CLM pipeline, Nexgn drastically reduces legal review cycles and eliminates signer friction.

## 1. AI Agreement Summary (Combating Contract Fatigue)
**The Problem:** Signers frequently face "contract fatigue," blindingly signing 40-page PDFs without reading them, leading to downstream legal disputes and compliance failures.
**The Nexgn AI Solution:** Nexgn AI analyzes the document envelope and generates a plain-language, bulleted executive summary of key terms, financial obligations, and critical dates. 
*   **Impact:** This summary is presented to the signer *before* execution, ensuring informed consent and reducing time-to-sign metrics by up to 60%.

## 2. AI Template Builder
**The Problem:** Drafting legal templates from scratch requires expensive external counsel and hours of manual field formatting.
**The Nexgn AI Solution:** Users input natural language prompts into the Nexgn Workspace (e.g., *"Generate a bilateral NDA for a software development agency based in India, governing intellectual property transfer"*). 
*   **Impact:** Nexgn AI instantly drafts the document, applies the appropriate legal terminology for the requested jurisdiction (e.g., IT Act 2000 compliance), and automatically maps drag-and-drop signature, text, and date fields to the generated layout.

## 3. AI Clause Assistant (Risk & Anomaly Detection)
During document preparation, the AI Clause Assistant actively scans the uploaded PDF or DOCX contract for anomalous, predatory, or non-standard clauses. 
*   **Risk Mitigation:** Flags high-risk indemnification clauses or asymmetrical liability limits.
*   **Boilerplate Checks:** Highlights missing standard boilerplate (e.g., Severability, Force Majeure, Governing Law).
*   **Smart Suggestions:** Suggests compliant, alternative phrasing based on modern legal standards.

## 4. Zero-Data-Retention & Privacy Policy
Nexgn operates on a strict **Zero-Data-Retention** policy for AI processing.
*   Customer documents, Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and signatures processed through Nexgn AI are **never** used to train foundational AI models. 
*   All AI endpoints utilize isolated, ephemeral inference containers, ensuring absolute enterprise data sovereignty. Nexgn AI is fully compliant with the EU GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.
