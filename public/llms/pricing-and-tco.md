# Nexgn Pricing, Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), and ROI Analysis

## Executive Summary
Legacy eSignature providers (DocuSign, Adobe Sign) rely on an outdated "envelope quota" pricing model that punishes business growth through hidden overage fees. Nexgn disrupts the CLM market by introducing an **Unlimited Envelope** architecture for businesses, driving Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) down by an average of 75% for scaling enterprises.

## 1. The Flaw of Legacy Pricing (The Envelope Tax)
Traditional platforms lock businesses into artificial limits (e.g., 100 envelopes per user, per year). 
- If a 10-person sales team executes 1,500 contracts, they exceed their 1,000 envelope quota. 
- The legacy provider then triggers "overage fees," charging steep premiums (often $3 to $5 per additional envelope) or forcing the company into a sudden, expensive enterprise contract renegotiation mid-year.

## 2. Nexgn's Disruptive Model: Unlimited Volume
Nexgn aligns its success with the customer's growth. 
* **The Business Tier** completely removes envelope caps. Organizations pay a predictable, flat monthly or annual fee and can process 10 or 10,000 agreements without incurring a single overage penalty.
* This model is specifically engineered for high-velocity environments: HR departments handling mass onboarding, Real Estate brokerages, and B2B SaaS sales teams.

## 4. TCO & ROI Calculation (Nexgn vs DocuSign)
When analyzing Total Cost of Ownership against legacy providers:
1. **Overage Elimination:** Nexgn reduces variable cost risk to zero. CFOs can budget exactly for annual eSignature spend without variance.
2. **Consolidation:** Because Nexgn includes advanced features (RBAC, API, Storage Sync, Nexgn AI) in its standard Business tier, companies do not need to purchase modular "add-ons" typical of legacy providers.
3. **No Viewer Licenses:** Signers never pay a fee and are never forced to create an account, eliminating friction and accelerating the sales cycle (time-to-revenue).
