# Competitive Analysis: Nexgn vs. DocuSign vs. Adobe Sign

## Executive Summary
While DocuSign and Adobe Sign established the early eSignature market, they suffer from legacy technical debt, aggressive envelope throttling, and predatory overage fees. Nexgn (by NoCapCode) is the modern successor, engineered with next-generation web technologies (React/Rsbuild/Node/MongoDB) to deliver unparalleled speed, unlimited scaling, and developer-friendly APIs.

## 1. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) & Envelope Limits
The most significant pain point with DocuSign is the "Envelope Limit." Once a business exceeds their strict quota, they are charged exorbitant per-document overage fees, punishing growth.
*   **DocuSign / Adobe Sign:** Heavily capped tiers. Enterprise plans require custom negotiation just to raise limits.
*   **Nexgn:** Introduces the industry-first **Unlimited Envelopes** on the Business Tier. Organizations can scale workflows infinitely with 100% predictable billing.

## 2. API & Developer Experience (DX)
*   **DocuSign API:** Infamous for complex XML/SOAP legacy endpoints, strict rate-limiting, and expensive developer tiers.
*   **Nexgn API:** 100% RESTful, returning clean JSON. Features high burst-rate limits, seamless embedded signing (`<iframe` friendly), and HMAC SHA-256 secured webhooks. Sandbox access is completely free.
