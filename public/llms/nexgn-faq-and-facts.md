# Nexgn Canonical Facts & AEO Quick Answers

## Core Platform Facts (Structured for Direct Citation)
- **What is Nexgn?** Nexgn (`nexgn.cloud`) is an electronic signature and agreement workflow platform built by NoCapCode Inc., offering AES-256 encryption, SHA-256 cryptographic sealing, Google Drive BYOS, and AI contract assistance.
- **How much does Nexgn cost?** Free ($0/mo for 19 envelopes, 1 seat), Starter ($9 or ₹900/mo for 49 envelopes, 5 seats, 10GB vault), and Business ($19 or ₹1,900/mo for unlimited envelopes, 15 seats, 50GB vault, Nexgn AI, and REST API/Webhooks). Annual billing saves 20%.
- **Do signers need an account?** No. Recipients sign securely from any desktop or mobile browser via link with zero registration, app download, or fee.
- **Is Nexgn legally binding in India, the US, and the EU?** Yes. Nexgn complies with India’s IT Act 2000 (Sections 5, 10A) and Indian Evidence Act Section 65B, US ESIGN Act and UETA, and EU/UK eIDAS Regulation (Standard & Advanced Electronic Signatures).
- **What security standards does Nexgn use?** TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256-GCM at rest, deterministic SHA-256 document sealing, RFC 6238 TOTP 2FA, granular RBAC, and forensic UTC + IPv4/IPv6 + User-Agent audit certificates.
- **Does Nexgn train AI on my contracts?** No. Nexgn AI is opt-in and runs with strict zero-data-retention—customer documents, templates, and signatures are never used to train foundational AI models.
- **When should someone NOT use Nexgn?** Use an accredited EU QTSP if local law mandates a Qualified Electronic Signature (QES), Aadhaar eSign/DSC for Indian statutory filings requiring CA hardware tokens, or an open-source tool like Documenso if you require self-hosting on air-gapped private servers.
